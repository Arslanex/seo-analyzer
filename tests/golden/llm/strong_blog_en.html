How I cut SaaS churn from 7% to 3.9% in 8 months

By Maria Chen, Head of Customer Success at Northwind Analytics. Last updated 30 September 2026.

Last year our monthly churn sat at 7%. I ran a retention programme myself, talked to 62 churned customers on the phone, and tracked every change in a spreadsheet. This post documents exactly what I did and what the numbers did afterwards.

What I tested

I started with a simple hypothesis: customers leave in the first 30 days because they never reach their first report. In January I personally rewrote the onboarding emails and added a 15-minute setup call for every new account. In my experience the call mattered more than any email.

The method, step by step

First, I exported all cancellations from 2025 and tagged each with a reason. Second, I sorted them by days-since-signup. Third, I picked the three biggest clusters and wrote one intervention for each. Fourth, I ran each intervention on a 20% cohort for six weeks before rolling it out.

Results

Before the programme, 30-day activation was 41%. After eight months it was 63%, and monthly churn fell from 7.0% to 3.9%. The setup call alone lifted activation by 11 points in the test cohort of 148 accounts. One account, a logistics firm in Rotterdam, told me on the call that they had nearly cancelled because nobody explained the export feature; after the call they upgraded to the team plan within a week.

Sources and further reading

The benchmark figures come from the 2025 SaaS Benchmarks report and the activation framework from this peer-reviewed study. I hold a Customer Success Manager certification and have worked in subscription businesses for ten years.

Customers say it best: "The setup call saved our rollout," wrote one account owner in their review.
